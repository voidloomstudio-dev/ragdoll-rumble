# RAGDOLL RUMBLE — Production Foundation

**Version:** 1.0.0 Foundation
**Engine:** Godot 4.7.2
**GitHub:** https://github.com/voidloomstudio-dev/ragdoll-rumble

## Implemented
- FPS/TPS camera (V key)
- Modular Weapon + WeaponData (pistol, shotgun, banana, hammer)
- Hybrid ragdoll states (NORMAL→HIT→STAGGER→PARTIAL→FULL→DEAD)
- 10 maps (arena, forest, farm, house, desert, snow, factory, rooftop, swamp, scifi)
- Animated dolls (run/jump/shoot/fight; ragdoll on KO only)
- VFXManager (muzzle, impact, damage flash)
- Mobile: dual sticks + FIRE/JUMP/RLD/MELEE/WPN
- Android joystick fix

## Controls
WASD Mouse | LMB Fire | R Reload | 1-4 Weapons | F Melee | V FPS/TPS | Q Ability | M Map

## Note
Foundation not full commercial content: Skeleton3D+AnimationTree library and unique weapon GLBs are next pipeline steps.
